# Images Directory

Place family-approved wedding photography and local artwork in this directory.

## Recommended Specifications

### 1. Hero Couple Portrait (`hero-couple.jpg`)
- **Orientation:** Portrait (3:4 or 4:5 aspect ratio)
- **Recommended Dimensions:** At least `1200 x 1600 px`
- **Focus:** Centered or upper-third composition for clean display on mobile screens and desktop side-by-side layouts.

### 2. Secondary Couple / Story Photo (`couple-story.jpg`)
- **Orientation:** Landscape (16:9) or Square (1:1)
- **Recommended Dimensions:** At least `1200 x 800 px` (landscape) or `1000 x 1000 px` (square)

### 3. Venue Reference (`venue.jpg` - Optional)
- **Orientation:** Landscape (16:9 or 3:2)
- **Recommended Dimensions:** At least `1200 x 800 px`

## Notes on Permissions and Privacy
- Confirm permission from the couple and family before publishing any photograph.
- Do not check private family photos or uncompressed raw camera files into public repositories.
- Keep file sizes optimized for the web (WebP or JPEG under 400KB per image).
- When real images are added, update `src/data/wedding.ts` to reference the filenames.
